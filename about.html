About me

I am a data modeler and analyst based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. I build data systems that hold up to scrutiny, including Scalable Brain, my own data and trading research platform. As a Data Analyst, I currently work with the Together Design Lab at Toronto Metropolitan University, where I lead analysis for a multi-institution research study — building reproducible cleaning pipelines, interactive geospatial maps, and verification tooling that ensures every published figure is traceable back to source data.

My background bridges engineering and analytics: a Bachelor of Electrical Engineering from the University of Nigeria and a Diploma in IT Data Analytics from NSCC. Along the way I have analyzed utility infrastructure data at Detect Technologies, supported housing research at NewRoots, and built tools spanning quantitative finance, machine learning, and the web. I work primarily in Python, R, and SQL, with Power BI, Tableau, ArcGIS, and ETL workflows alongside.

I am drawn to problems where the analysis has to hold up to scrutiny and the result has to be usable by someone who is not an analyst. That interest currently shows up in Grid Power Energy, a techno-economic and policy study of distributed versus centralized electricity models in Nova Scotia, and in recognition from the Arthur L. Irving Entrepreneurship Centre and NSCC for accessibility- and user-focused innovation.
